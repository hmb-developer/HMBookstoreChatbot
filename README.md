
# HM Book Store Chatbot

## Overview

HM Book Store Chatbot is an intelligent customer support assistant designed to enhance the online shopping experience for customers of HM Book Store. The chatbot automates customer service, assists users in finding books, provides personalized recommendations, tracks orders, and answers frequently asked questions.

The project aims to improve customer satisfaction, streamline support operations, and increase sales by guiding customers toward informed purchasing decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

- Develop a chatbot capable of handling customer inquiries and requests effectively.
- Provide 24/7 automated support for common questions and issues.
- Assist customers with book discovery and personalized recommendations.
- Enable customers to track orders and shipping status.
- Increase operational efficiency and boost sales revenue.

---

## Key Features

- Personalized greetings based on time of day.
- Advanced book search by title, author, ISBN, keywords, and genre.
- Personalized book recommendations based on user behavior and popular titles.
- Real-time order status tracking.
- Customer support escalation to email or live chat.
- Display of store operating hours and holiday schedules.
- Promotion and offer notifications.
- FAQ responses using a searchable knowledge base.
- Multilingual support in English and Spanish.

## Project Scope

The chatbot will be integrated into the HM Book Store website and will support customers throughout their purchasing journey. It will offer search, recommendations, support, order tracking, promotions, and multilingual capabilities.
